What the score means

The badge is a local heuristic built from artifact validity, subject cues, detail, latency and stream behavior. It is a useful signal, not proof of model identity. A poor score can also come from a bad prompt response, a truncated stream or a channel that does not support tools. Always confirm billing and model claims with the provider.
